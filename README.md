# Renewable Energy Land Survey & Mapping

## Project Overview

This project presents a GPS-based land surveying and mapping workflow developed for land assessment and preliminary planning of renewable energy projects, particularly solar and wind energy applications.

The workflow integrates field data collection using Hand GPS with MapSource for GPS data management, Google Earth Pro and Global Mapper for geospatial analysis, and AutoCAD for survey drafting and mapping.

## Objectives

- To identify and assess suitable land for renewable energy projects.
- To collect and manage geographical coordinates using GPS-based surveying.
- To verify land boundaries and assess surveyed land areas.
- To analyze satellite imagery and surrounding site features.
- To support access route and pathway planning.
- To prepare survey layouts and mapping drawings using AutoCAD.
- To organize survey information for project documentation and planning.

## Survey & Mapping Workflow

**Land Identification → GPS Field Survey → GPS Data Processing → Geospatial Analysis → Area & Boundary Assessment → AutoCAD Drafting → Final Survey Documentation**

## Equipment

## Methodology

### 1. Land Identification

The project area is initially reviewed to identify suitable land parcels and understand surrounding geographical features.

### 2. GPS Field Survey

Hand GPS is used to collect geographical coordinates and identify relevant land boundaries, locations and access routes.

### 3. GPS Data Processing

Survey data is transferred and organized using Garmin MapSource for coordinate and track management.

### 4. Geospatial Analysis

Google Earth Pro and Global Mapper are used for satellite-based analysis, site verification, route assessment and interpretation of geographical features.

### 5. Area & Boundary Assessment

Survey coordinates are used to assess land boundaries and calculate the surveyed land area.

### 6. AutoCAD Drafting

Survey data is used to prepare boundary layouts, coordinate-based drawings, route alignments and survey maps using AutoCAD.

### 7. Documentation

The processed survey information, maps, observations and supporting documentation are organized for project planning and future reference.

## Advantages

- Systematic approach to land surveying and mapping.
- Integration of field survey data with geospatial tools.
- Supports efficient land assessment and preliminary project planning.
- Helps organize survey information for technical documentation.
- Provides a digital workflow from field data collection to survey drawing preparation.

## Limitations

- GPS accuracy depends on equipment, satellite availability and field conditions.
- Dense vegetation and physical obstructions may affect GPS observations.
- Satellite imagery may not always represent current site conditions.
- Detailed engineering decisions require appropriate site-specific surveys and verification.

# Survey Methodology

This section describes the field surveying and mapping methodology used for renewable energy land assessment.

## Survey Workflow

1. Site identification and preliminary study
2. GPS-based field data collection
3. Boundary and location verification
4. Coordinate data transfer and processing
5. Google Earth Pro analysis
6. Area calculation and land assessment
7. AutoCAD-based survey drafting
8. Final map preparation and verification

## Field Survey

Hand GPS was used to collect geographic coordinates and identify land boundaries, access routes, and important site features.

## Data Processing

GPS data was transferred and processed using MapSource for coordinate management and further mapping activities.

## Mapping and Verification

Google Earth Pro was used for satellite-based site analysis, route assessment, geographical verification, and comparison with field survey information.

## AutoCAD Drafting

AutoCAD was used to prepare survey drawings, boundary layouts, coordinate-based maps, area calculations, and route alignment drawings.

## Output

The methodology produces organized survey data, land maps, boundary information, area assessments, and route planning drawings suitable for renewable energy project planning.

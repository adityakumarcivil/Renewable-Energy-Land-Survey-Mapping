# Renewable Energy Land Survey & Mapping

## Project Overview

This project presents a GPS-based land surveying and mapping workflow developed for renewable energy applications, particularly solar and wind energy projects.

The project integrates field data collection using Hand GPS with MapSource data processing, Google Earth Pro analysis, and AutoCAD drafting to support land identification, boundary verification, area assessment, route planning, and preparation of survey drawings.

## Objectives

- To identify and assess suitable land for renewable energy projects.
- To collect accurate geographical coordinates using Hand GPS.
- To process and organize GPS survey data using MapSource.
- To analyze surveyed land and surrounding features using Google Earth Pro.
- To prepare survey layouts and drawings using AutoCAD.
- To calculate and verify land areas using survey data.
- To support route and access pathway planning for project development.

## Study Area

The methodology can be applied to land parcels considered for solar and wind energy projects, including areas requiring boundary identification, topographical assessment, access route planning, and land area verification.

## Methodology

The overall workflow consists of the following stages:

1. Preliminary study and land identification.
2. GPS-based field data collection.
3. Transfer and processing of GPS data using MapSource.
4. Satellite-based analysis using Google Earth Pro.
5. Coordinate plotting and survey drafting in AutoCAD.
6. Land area calculation and boundary verification.
7. Preparation of final survey maps and project documentation.

## Advantages

- Provides organized and systematic survey data.
- Supports accurate land area assessment.
- Helps in preliminary project planning.
- Integrates field survey data with satellite imagery.
- Reduces manual effort in map preparation and data verification.

## Limitations

- GPS accuracy depends on the equipment and field conditions.
- Dense vegetation and obstructions may affect GPS observations.
- Google Earth imagery may not always represent current site conditions.
- Final engineering decisions require detailed surveys and site verification.

## Future Scope

The project can be further enhanced by integrating GIS platforms, advanced GNSS surveying, drone-based mapping, digital elevation models, and Python-based automation for survey data processing and analysis.
